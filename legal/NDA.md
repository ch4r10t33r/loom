## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that Recipient can

(a) was lawfully in Recipient's possession without a duty of confidence before
disclosure by Discloser;
(b) is or becomes public through no act or omission of Recipient;
(c) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence; or
(d) is independently developed by Recipient without any use of or reference to
the Confidential Information.

Recipient shall:
(a) hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence, using at least a
reasonable degree of care;

(c) not disclose it to any third party without Discloser's prior written consent;
