# Pre-Gating Without Retraining

The research paper behind loom's pre-gate predictor (`--pregate-head`) and
research lever 9. `main.pdf` is the compiled paper; `main.tex` + `refs.bib`
are the source.

Build: `tectonic main.tex` (or any LaTeX with bibtex; unsrtnat style).

Every reported number has a dated row in the whitepaper's Appendix A
decision log. Scripts behind each experiment are in `../scripts/`; trained
heads, router checkpoints, and activation traces are attached to the
v0.40.3 release.
